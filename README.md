# 🌍 Global Store Sales Analysis: Decoding Worldwide E-Commerce Trends
*A deep-dive visualization project handling massive transnational retail datasets to explore sales efficiency, shipping costs, and global profit distributions.*

## 📖 The "Why" behind this Project
In global e-commerce, complexity scales exponentially. You aren't just tracking products; you are tracking multiple currencies, shipping constraints across diverse regions, and vastly different customer purchasing behaviors. 
This project simulates the environment of a Fortune 500 company. It demonstrates my ability to wrangle massive, multi-regional datasets and condense them into an actionable global dashboard highly sought after by enterprise decision-makers and stakeholders.

Designed to give a "Control Room" view of the global business landscape:
- **Global Heatmapping:** Utilizing geospatial data to map revenue density across North America, EMEA, and APAC regions.
- **Shipping Logistics Optimization:** Analyzing the correlation between different shipping modalities (Standard vs. Expedited) and overall profit margins.
- **Category Matrix:** Breaking down macro-categories (Technology, Furniture, Office Supplies) into granular SKU-level performance metrics.
- **Time-Series Analysis:** Uncovering cyclical buying patterns (e.g., Holiday spikes) over multi-year scales.

---

## 💡 Business Impact & Value
- **Strategic Expansion:** Pinpointing highly profitable but low-volume regions that are ripe for marketing blitzes.
- **Cost Reduction:** Identifying regions where shipping costs are cannibalizing profit margins, highlighting a need for supply-chain negotiation.
- **Inventory Management:** Forecasting demand spikes based on historical multi-year trends, preventing stock-outs during critical shopping seasons.

## 🛠️ Technical Arsenal
- **Data Engineering:** Extracting, transforming, and loading complex datasets containing thousands of rows and dozens of columns using Power Query.
- **Data Modeling:** Creating relational models to connect shipping tables, order tables, and customer demographic tables.
- **Visualization:** Microsoft Power BI, Geospatial mapping, custom DAX metrics for real-time currency/profit calculations.
